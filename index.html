Welcome to NoteSquid!

Have you ever felt overwhelmed by the amount of things you have to do? Do you find yourself forgetting important tasks or deadlines? Would you say that you're agenda feels messy and unorganized? If so, NoteSquid is here to help! NoteSquid is a web application that helps its users with productivity. It allows the user to create checklists, take notes, assign due dates to things, and set reminders. Not only that, NoteSquid has an extremely user-friendly interface that makes organization easy and convenient! Click on any of the buttons below to proceed to the page they are labled as. Don't forget to create or log into an account to save your data! Do so by clicking the "Account" button.
