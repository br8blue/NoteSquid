Checklists

This is the checklists page, where you can create and manage your checklists. Click the plus button to create a new checklist. You will be granted a text box, which is where you type in said checklist. Click enter while in the text box to create a new checkbox. You can click on the checkboxes as you do your tasks to mark them as complete. You can assign your checklists with headers, and click the up arrow button in the text box to save it permanently.
